Ganesha 2025 · Thursday, 21 August

गणपती बाप्पा मोरयाThis year, we made our own Ganesha.

A week before Ganesh Chaturthi, the office floor became a studio. Newspapers went down, natural clay came out, and by evening seven hand-shaped Ganeshas sat on the front desk.

Natural clay

Every idol is unbaked earth. At visarjan it dissolves in a bucket of water at home, and the clay can go back into a plant pot.

No plaster, no paint run-off

Skipping plaster of Paris and chemical paints keeps lakes and drains clear after the festival.

Made side by side

Analysts, engineers and ops sat on one floor, swapped tips across the circle and each left with something they made.
